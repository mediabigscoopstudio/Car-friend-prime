About CarFriend

We built CarFriend because selling a used car in India meant one lowball offer at a time. We wanted a real bidding war instead — and zero commission for the seller who made it happen.

Our story

CarFriend started with a simple frustration: every seller we knew got one offer from one yard, took it or left it, and never found out what their car was actually worth to the market. Meanwhile dealers were driving city to city chasing inventory, sight-unseen, wasting a full day on cars they'd never bid on.

So we built a guided, phone-based self-inspection that takes about eleven minutes, and put the resulting listing in front of every verified dealer in the city at once — live, competing, in real time. If the auction still doesn't clear a fair price, One Click Buy guarantees a sale anyway. No commission, no hidden fee, and the seller decides every step.

Our mission

Give every car seller in India the same thing a dealer already has: real-time visibility into what the market will actually pay, and the leverage of competition to get it.
