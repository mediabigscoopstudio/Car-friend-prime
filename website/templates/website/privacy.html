Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use CarFriend as a seller or dealer, we collect information you provide directly, including your name, phone number, email address, city/state, and vehicle details submitted during listing or self-inspection. Dealers additionally provide company and dealership license details. KYC verification (via our provider, Surepass) may involve Aadhaar, PAN, and vehicle registration data.

2. How we use your information

  • To verify your identity and eligibility to list, bid, or subscribe.
  • To operate auctions, One Click Buy flows, and dealer listings.
  • To send notifications about bids, auction status, and account activity.
  • To process subscription billing through Razorpay.
  • To improve platform safety and prevent fraud.

3. What we don't do

CarFriend never processes payment or RC transfer between a seller and a dealer — those happen directly between the two parties, off-platform. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data retention

We retain account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at privacy@carfriend.in. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required by applicable law.

6. Data security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

7. Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to operate CarFriend, including AWS (hosting and storage), Razorpay (payments for dealer subscriptions), and Surepass (KYC and vehicle verification). Each is bound by its own privacy and security obligations.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the app or by email.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@carfriend.in.
